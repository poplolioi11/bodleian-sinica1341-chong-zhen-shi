# 崇真實棄假謊略說（1816 年馬六甲刊，牛津大學 Sinica 1341） / Chong zhen shi qi jia huang lüe shuo [Tract on the Sin of Lying and the Importance of Truth]: Transcription Dataset

米憐（William Milne）署「博愛者」撰《崇真實棄假謊略說》，1816 年在馬六甲刊行，原為《察世俗每月統記傳》的一篇。文中以招攬外地客商、約定還款、上公堂作證等場景談謊言與互信，可供華文期刊史與傳教出版研究對照。底本為牛津大學博德利圖書館 Sinica 1341 抽印本。

William Milne’s vernacular Chinese tract on lying and truthfulness was published in Malacca in 1816 under the pen name Bo’ai zhe. The pamphlet discusses sales to visiting customers, promises to repay debts, and testimony before officials. This transcription follows the Bodleian Libraries’ offprint from the Chinese Monthly Magazine, Sinica 1341.

## V0.2.0

16 幅有原圖定位，收第3幅題葉及第5至14幅正文全 10 面。另記封面、藏印、經文旁注、版心與文字旁線。比對獨立原圖讀法後回圖裁定，採用 9 筆訂正，留存疑讀定位。

All 16 images are indexed. Text covers the title leaf on scan 3 and all ten body faces on scans 5–14. Wrappers, seals, marginal scripture references, running titles and name lines are documented. An independent image reading was compared with the first transcription. Source-image adjudication adopted 9 changes and retained unresolved readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。原刻圈點與欄外經文分層記錄，留底本所見異體字和未定字。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Printed marks, marginal scripture references, original forms and uncertain readings are distinguished. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
